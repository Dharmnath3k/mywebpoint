FAQs

Common Questions

Are these prices fixed?

These are starting prices. Final cost depends on the complexity, features, and scope of your project. We provide a detailed quote after the free discovery call.

What's included in 'support'?

Post-launch support includes bug fixes, minor content updates, and technical assistance. It does not include new features or design changes.

Do you offer payment in instalments?

Yes. We typically work on a 40% advance, 40% at mid-project milestone, and 20% at delivery model. Custom payment plans are available for larger projects.

How do I get started?

Simply click 'Get Started' or 'Contact Us' and fill out the enquiry form. We'll schedule a free discovery call within 24 hours.

Can I upgrade from Starter to Professional later?

Absolutely. We design all projects with future scalability in mind, making upgrades smooth and cost-effective.
